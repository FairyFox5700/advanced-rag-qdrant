Northwind Robotics was founded in 2017 by Elena Vasquez and Marcus Chen in Seattle,
Washington. The company specializes in autonomous warehouse robots and builds its
core product line, the Sentinel series, for logistics automation.

Elena Vasquez serves as CEO and previously worked as a robotics researcher at the
University of Washington. Marcus Chen serves as CTO and led the original design of
the Sentinel-1 navigation stack. Before founding Northwind Robotics, Marcus Chen
worked at Boston Dynamics on legged locomotion control systems.

In 2019, Northwind Robotics partnered with Cascade Logistics to deploy the first
fleet of 50 Sentinel-1 robots at a distribution center in Tacoma. The partnership
with Cascade Logistics was considered a turning point for the company, proving that
autonomous robots could operate safely alongside human workers in a live warehouse.

By 2021, Northwind Robotics had raised a Series B funding round led by Highline
Ventures, with participation from Cascade Logistics as a strategic investor. The
funding was used to scale manufacturing of the Sentinel-2, an upgraded robot with
improved battery life and a redesigned gripper arm.

Northwind Robotics is headquartered in Seattle but operates a manufacturing plant in
Portland, Oregon. The Portland plant assembles both the Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2
product lines and employs approximately 300 people.
