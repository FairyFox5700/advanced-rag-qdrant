Cascade Logistics is a regional logistics and fulfillment company headquartered in
Tacoma, Washington. Founded in 2005 by Robert Nguyen, Cascade Logistics operates
distribution centers across the Pacific Northwest and specializes in same-day
delivery for e-commerce retailers.

In 2019, Cascade Logistics became the first customer of Northwind Robotics' Sentinel-1
robots, deploying an initial fleet of 50 units at its Tacoma distribution center. The
deployment reduced average pallet transport time by 40 percent within the first year.

Following the success of the pilot, Cascade Logistics expanded its partnership with
Northwind Robotics in 2021, both as a customer purchasing Sentinel-2 units and as a
strategic investor in Northwind Robotics' Series B funding round led by Highline
Ventures.

Robert Nguyen sits on Northwind Robotics' advisory board as a result of this
partnership, providing operational feedback that shaped the safety-rated laser
scanner added to the Sentinel-2.

Highline Ventures is a Seattle-based venture capital firm that focuses on robotics
and industrial automation startups. In addition to Northwind Robotics, Highline
Ventures has invested in several other logistics-technology companies in the Pacific
Northwest.
